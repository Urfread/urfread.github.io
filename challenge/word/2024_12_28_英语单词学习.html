Look at the crystal chandelier glittering. It's so beautiful!

He is overstating what really happened to gain people's sympathy.

锅中的水正在蒸发。

The water in the pot is evaporating.

His teeth gleamed after using this special whitening toothpaste.

The huge refrigerator has lots of storage space!

这家书店的书是按体裁来分类排放的。

冰川由大冰块组成。

The glacier is made up of big blocks of ice.

他测量了自己的身高 —— 他有 2 米高！

He measures himself. He is 2 meters tall!

由于生意不景气，他不得不考虑裁员。

Due to a downturn in business, he had to consider a reduction in staff numbers.

在冬天，政府会给生活艰苦的人们发放免费的汤。

The government gives out free soup to the needy in winter.
